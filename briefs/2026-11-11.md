- **Exposed:** REITs, homebuilders (Lennar, D.R. Horton), leveraged corporates. Banks benefit from NIM expansion.
- **Timeline:** Imminent — Dec FOMC is 5 weeks out. PPI on Thursday will either confirm or soften the CPI signal.
- **Priced in:** Partially — 77% Dec hike probability still leaves room for further repricing if PPI also surprises.

**2. Iran / Strait of Hormuz**
- **Transmission:** Oil supply risk premium → energy prices → global inflation expectations → central bank reaction functions.
- **Exposed:** Airlines (fuel costs), chemicals (naphtha), Asian importers. Energy equities benefit but with extreme volatility.
- **Least exposed:** Tech/software with no commodity input costs.
- **Timeline:** Structural risk; peace talks ongoing but ceasefire fragile. Both Gulf chokepoints were impaired simultaneously earlier in 2026 — historically rare.
- **Priced in:** Partially — Brent at ~$106 reflects a significant geopolitical premium but could spike to $120+ if Hormuz traffic is disrupted again.

**3. Post-Midterm Fiscal/Policy Shift**
- **Transmission:** Democratic Congress vs (presumably) Republican White House → gridlock on tax cuts, but potential bipartisan infrastructure spend, defense reauthorization.
- **Exposed:** Defense contractors, infrastructure/construction (positive); pharma (drug pricing risk), big tech (regulatory scrutiny).
- **Timeline:** Weeks to months for legislative agenda to crystallize.
- **Priced in:** Initial relief rally priced in on Monday; longer-term policy implications are not.

**Second-order effect:** If Congress pivots to fiscal expansion while the Fed is still tightening, the policy mix clash could steepen the yield curve further — positive for bank earnings, negative for long-duration assets.
